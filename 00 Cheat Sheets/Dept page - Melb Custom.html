The Revision Chain

How a single photo becomes a client-approved concept in three prompts

STEP 1 — ELEMENT SWAP

Prompt:

"change the stools of the 1st pic with the stool in the 2nd pic"

Multi-reference mode (2/5 images). ReLook pulls the gold bar stools from one photo and places them into the VRC pavilion interior. Premium 2K model.

STEP 2 — SKETCH INPAINT

Prompt:

"raise the height of the curved wall as per the pink sketch"

Draw directly on the image with pink overlay to indicate structural changes. The AI interprets your sketch as architectural intent — no 3D modelling needed.

STEP 3 — MATERIAL FINISH

Prompt:

"change all the metal finishing (background and base object) to black metal matte"

Global material swap across the entire scene. One prompt changes every metal surface from gold to matte black — instant client revision without re-rendering.

Total time for 3 revisions: ~2 minutes vs. 2-3 weeks traditional
