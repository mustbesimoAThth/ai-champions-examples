Production Use Cases

Where ReLook fits into the production workflow

Lighting Visualisation

Add realistic light beams to CAD drawings. Define zones, control spill, show clients exactly what the lighting design will look like — before a single fixture is hung.

Rigging & Truss Previews

Swap truss finishes (Silver HD44 vs Black HD34), add draping, visualise flying elements — all from the Vectorworks drawing without going back to 3D software.

CAD ↔ Realistic Conversion

Turn flat technical drawings into photorealistic renders for client decks. Or go the other way — generate clean 2D elevations from site photos for documentation.

Production Pack Visuals

Generate hero images for production packs that sit alongside your technical specs. Clients see the vision and the detail — in the same document.
