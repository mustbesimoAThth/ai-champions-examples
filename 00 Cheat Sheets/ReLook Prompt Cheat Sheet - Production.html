Prompt patterns for production visualisation — lighting, rigging, and set design. Upload your Vectorworks drawing, sketch what you need, and generate. Platform: relook.hthsystems.com.au

Lighting & Effects (Sketch Inpaint Mode)
Add Light Beams
"Generate a light beam where the pink arrows are"
Draw pink arrows on CAD view pointing where beams should hit — actual prompt used
Controlled Beam Zones
"Generate light beams where the pink arrows are to illuminate the green grid on the floor, avoid the light to go beyond the green line boundary"
Use coloured sketches to define lighting zones and boundaries
Spotlight Array
"Add [warm white / cool white / coloured] spotlight beams from each fixture on the truss, angled at the [stage / display / cars]"
Specify beam colour and temperature for accurate production vis
Atmospheric Haze
"Add atmospheric haze to make the light beams visible, with [subtle / dramatic] effect"
Haze makes beams visible in renders — essential for lighting presentations
Rigging & Truss Visualisation
Truss Finish Swap
"Change all truss from [silver / black] to [black HD34 / silver HD44 / white powder-coated]"
Match truss spec to actual stock — Silver HD44 vs Black HD34
Add Rigging Elements
"Add [chain motors / truss totems / ground support towers] to the marked positions in the sketch"
Sketch positions, prompt describes what goes there
Draping & Soft Goods
"Add [black draping / star cloth / scrim] hanging from the truss between the marked points"
For back-of-house masking or decorative elements on truss
Flying Elements
"Add a [LED screen / banner / signage panel] suspended from the truss at the position shown in the sketch"
Great for visualising hung elements before rigging

Venue & Set Design

Stage / Set Visualisation

Turn a bare CAD plan into a dressed, lit production visual
Power Tips
1
Colour-code your sketches. Pink = where things go. Green = boundaries/zones. The AI reads both colour and position.
2
Say "remove the sketch marks" in your prompt to get a clean final output without your overlay lines.
3
Be specific about beam type. "Narrow spot" vs "wash" vs "flood" gives very different results. Use production language.
4
Premium 2K for client-facing. Fast model for quick internal checks. Premium for anything going into a production pack.
5
Vectorworks exports work great. Export as JPEG from ISO or plan view — ReLook handles the rest.
6
Iterate on the lighting. V1 gets the beams. V2 refines direction and spread. Don't start from scratch.
