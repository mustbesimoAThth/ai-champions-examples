Master Prompt 2 — Material Interpolation (2-Image Method)
Apply Materials from One Photo onto Another Structure (Actual Prompt Used)
Image 1 = The booth sketch or structure you want to render Image 2 = A real booth photo whose materials/finishes you want to apply

"Generate a photorealistic 3D render of the 1st pic using the materials of the 2nd pic"

The AI uses Image 1 as the structural blueprint and Image 2 as the material palette — extracting the finishes, textures, and lighting style and applying them to the new design. Use Premium 2K + Enhancer Ready for best results.
Booth Variations — Colour, Material & Branding Swaps
Colour / Finish Variation
"Show the same booth in [navy blue / charcoal / white gloss / timber natural] — keep the structure and layout identical"
Used for Aspen booth variations — generate 3–5 colourways from one render
Brand Swap — New Client
"Re-brand this booth for [new client name] using their brand colours [colour 1] and [colour 2] — replace all logos and fascia text"
Same physical stand, new client — fast pitch visual without rebuilding
Remove All Branding
"Remove all logos, brand text, and signage from this booth. Show a clean, neutral stand ready for re-branding"
Creates a blank template for re-use across multiple client pitches
Panel / Fascia Material
"Change the booth panels from [current finish] to [gloss white / brushed aluminium / dark timber veneer / fabric wrap]"
Material changes dramatically shift the perceived quality — generate before showing client
Before → After & Targeted Fixes
Before → After (Site Photo)
"Take this photo of the raw or partially built stand and show it as a finished, lit, fully dressed exhibition booth"
Turns a bump-in site photo into a finished client-facing visual
Add Lighting to Existing
"Add integrated LED strip lighting to the fascia edges, warm spotlights on the display areas, and backlit signage panels"
Brightens a flat booth photo — clients respond strongly to lit renders
Inpaint — Fix Specific Area
"Change only the [reception counter / rear wall / flooring] — paint over the area in pink and describe what replaces it"
Use inpaint mode for targeted changes without regenerating the full booth
Add People / Activate Space
"Add professional visitors in business attire interacting with the stand — some at reception, some at demo pods, none overcrowding"
Activating an empty booth makes proposals feel real — essential for client decks
Power Tips
1
Sketch quality doesn't matter. Even a rough hand sketch works as Image 1. The AI interprets intent — not line quality.
2
Material interpolation is your superpower. Upload any booth you love as Image 2 and the AI copies its finishes onto your new design.
3
Name materials specifically. "Matte white laminated panel" beats "white wall". "Brushed aluminium trim" beats "metal". Precision = accuracy.
4
Premium 2K always for client-facing booth renders. Fast model for quick internal checks only.
5
Generate a "no branding" base first. Then apply client branding as a second prompt — easier to pivot when the client changes brief.
6
Variations in one session. V1 = structure. V2 = different colour. V3 = branding applied. Each builds on the last — don't restart.
