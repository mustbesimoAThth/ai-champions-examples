Master Prompt: Polestar Exhibition

The proven prompt structure for hyper-realistic exhibition renders

Structure Lock Image 1 defines everything. Do not change, move, scale, or redesign: stand layout, car positions, camera angle, signage placement, overall composition.
Vehicles Use real production models only — Polestar 2, 3, 4. Maintain exact positions and realistic proportions. No concept or altered designs.
Floor — Hard Rule Floor height: max 20mm only. Must appear visually flush. Not a stage, not raised. White glossy surface, soft reflections, thin metallic edge trim. If it looks raised → incorrect.
Lighting Black, minimal, architectural fittings. Warm-neutral white spotlights. Medium atmospheric haze to reveal beams. Soft, realistic beam falloff. No harsh theatrical cones.
No Additions No furniture. No barriers. No props. No extra signage. No decorative additions. Keep the scene minimal.
Camera Realism Natural lens behaviour. Soft highlight roll-off. Subtle micro-contrast. Realistic depth. Clean tonal gradients. No stylisation. No heavy effects.
⚠ Fail conditions: floor looks raised, cars aren't real models, lightbox missing, extra elements added
