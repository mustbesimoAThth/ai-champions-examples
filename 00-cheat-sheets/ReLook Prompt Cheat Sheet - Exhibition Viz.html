Master Prompt 1 — Hyper-Realistic Exhibition Render
Polestar Exhibition — Full Master Prompt
Image 1 = Concept layout (MASTER — preserved exactly) Image 2 = Lighting plan Image 3 = HDRI hall reference

Create a hyper-realistic Polestar exhibition render.

ABSOLUTE MASTER LOCK: Image 1 defines everything. Do not change, move, scale, or redesign: stand layout, car positions, camera angle, signage placement, overall composition.

VEHICLES: Real production models only — Polestar 2, 3, 4. Exact positions, realistic proportions.

FLOOR (HARD RULE): Max 20mm height. Visually flush with surrounding floor. White glossy surface, soft reflections, thin metallic edge trim. Subtle shadow line only. If it looks raised → incorrect.

NO ADDITIONS: No furniture, barriers, props, extra signage, or decorations. Keep minimal.

LIGHTING: Black minimal architectural fittings. Warm-neutral white spotlights. Medium atmospheric haze for visible beams. Soft beam falloff.

LIGHTBOX: Suspended Polestar lightbox — black structure, white logo, even illumination. Must remain.

PEOPLE: Walking naturally. Some crossing stand seamlessly (no step). Neutral modern clothing. Don't overcrowd.

ENVIRONMENT: Large modern exhibition hall. Clean architecture. HDRI-style lighting. Slight cool tone.

CAMERA REALISM: Natural lens behaviour, soft highlights, micro-contrast, realistic depth, clean gradients. No stylisation.

Quick Modifiers — Add to Either Prompt
Swap Product
"Replace [car/product] with a [specific model name and colour]"
Works for any brand — Zeekr 001, Leapmotor C11, etc.
Change Floor
"Change floor to [black glossy / branded vinyl / timber deck], keep flush 20mm height"
Always re-state the 20mm rule when changing floor
Adjust Lighting
"Change lighting colour to [cool white / warm amber / brand blue] with [subtle / dramatic] haze"
Specify beam colour + haze level for best results
Add Branding
"Add illuminated [CLIENT] logo on the [backwall / lightbox / reception counter]"
Specify position and whether the logo is backlit or surface-mounted
Fail Conditions — Check Every Render
Floor Check
Floor appears raised or looks like a stage — any visible step = fail
Most common error. Always check floor edge first.
Vehicle Check
Cars are not recognisable real models — generic or altered designs = fail
Must be identifiable production models
Lightbox Check
Suspended lightbox is missing, moved, or restyled = fail
Must match position and style from concept
Addition Check
Extra elements added (furniture, barriers, props) = fail
Scene must remain minimal unless explicitly requested
Power Tips
1
3-image combo is key. Concept + lighting plan + venue HDRI = the best results. Don't skip images.
2
Lock structure first. Always state "Image 1 defines everything" — prevents the AI from reinterpreting your layout.
3
State fail conditions. Explicitly listing what's wrong helps the AI avoid common mistakes.
4
Premium 2K for client-facing. Fast model for quick internal checks. Premium for anything going into a production pack.
5
Iterate, don't restart. V1 gets the structure. V2 refines lighting and materials. V3 nails the atmosphere.
6
Swap brands fast. Same prompt, different product + logo + colours = new client render in seconds.
