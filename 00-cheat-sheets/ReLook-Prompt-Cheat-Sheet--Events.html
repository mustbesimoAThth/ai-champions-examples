Proven prompt patterns for Sydney event visualization — from Randwick to the Harbour. Copy these, swap the specifics, and generate. Platform: relook.hthsystems.com.au

Colour & Sponsor Branding
Umbrella / Furniture Swap
"Change all umbrellas to [colour] and match the table cloths"
Works for: parasols, chair cushions, bar stools, ottomans — used for Randwick racing carnival
Sponsor Rebrand
"Replace all signage and branding with [brand name] in their brand colours [colour 1] and [colour 2]"
Upload a logo image as reference 2 for best results — great for pitch pivots
Full Colour Scheme
"Change the entire colour palette to [colour] and [colour], including furniture, draping, and accents"
Show a client 3 colourway options from the same venue shot in minutes
Structure / Marquee Re-skin
"Change the marquee exterior from [current colour / material] to [new brand colour / white stretch]"
Works on: pavilion exteriors, marquee covers, temporary walls, fencing wraps
Atmosphere & Time of Day
Golden Hour / Sunset
"Show this venue at golden hour with a dramatic sunset sky and warm ambient lighting"
Turns a flat Randwick or Homebush site visit into a stunning evening pitch
Night Event
"Convert to nighttime with festoon lighting, warm uplights on the structure, and a twilight sky"
Add "Sydney Harbour Bridge glow in the distance" for outdoor harbour events
Weather / Mood
"Show this with bright blue Sydney summer sky, sharp shadows, and a vibrant outdoor atmosphere"
NSW summer light is crisp — emphasise it for racing and tennis events
Season / Occasion
"Show this venue in spring racing colours — warm afternoon light, floral accents, and a dressed crowd atmosphere"
Useful for Everest, Flight Stakes, Golden Rose at Randwick pitch decks
Layout & Furniture Changes
Furniture Swap (Multi-Ref)
"Replace the [chairs / tables / stools] with the ones shown in reference image 2"
Upload a Folio product photo as reference — verified HTH stock placed directly into the scene
Add Event Elements
"Add a [bar area / DJ booth / stage / picnic area] to the [left / right / back / foreground] of the space"
Be specific about placement and scale for best results — reference a Folio item if possible
Dress the Venue
"Add round banquet tables with white linen, floral centrepieces, and bentwood chairs to fill the space"
Best for: empty venue → dressed gala transformation — works great for ICC Sydney ballroom shots
Remove Clutter
"Remove the [scaffolding / barriers / equipment / security fencing] and show a clean finished setup"
Clean up pre-event site photos for client-facing presentations
Sydney Venue Context — Quick Tricks
Harbour / Water Views
"Add a Sydney Harbour backdrop with the Bridge and Opera House visible in the background at dusk"
Powerful for outdoor waterfront event pitches — use inpainting to preserve the foreground
Racing Carnival Crowd
"Add a well-dressed crowd in spring racing attire — hats, fascinators, suit jackets — interacting naturally with the space"
Used for Everest and Randwick racing carnival client renders
Tennis Venue Setup
"Show this outdoor entertainment area dressed for a premium tennis hospitality event — white and green palette, sponsor signage, tiered seating in background"
Refs: HTH United Tennis Sydney — court-side hospitality setups
Corporate Outdoor Terrace
"Show this terrace dressed as a corporate cocktail event at sunset — high tables, bar service, city skyline backdrop"
Works for Darling Harbour, Barangaroo, and SMC Conference Centre pitches
Power Tips
1
Use "Enhancer Ready" — it auto-expands your prompt with professional photography terms. Your 5-word prompt becomes a 50-word masterpiece.
2
Multi-reference is your friend. Upload the venue photo + the HTH product photo you want placed in it. Up to 5 references total.
3
Iterate, don't start over. If V1 is 80% right, refine with a follow-up prompt. Don't re-upload and start from scratch — each version builds on the last.
4
Use Premium 2K for anything going into a client deck or proposal. Use Fast model for quick internal exploration.
5
Be specific about what stays. "Change the umbrellas to navy but keep everything else the same" prevents unwanted changes across the scene.
6
Studio Enhanced adds professional camera angles and composition. Great for hero shots — skip it if you need the exact original framing.
