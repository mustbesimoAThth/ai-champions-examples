The Actual Workflow

How ReLook Works

Three steps. Your photo. Your words. Your result.

1

Upload Your Photo

Take a site photo, a past event shot, or a venue visit snap. Upload up to 5 reference images.

Supports: JPG, PNG, WEBP up to 10MB

2
Your Prompt
"Change all umbrellas to lime green and make the building facade white"

Describe the Change

Write what you want in plain English. Colour swaps, material changes, atmosphere shifts, sponsor branding — just say it.

Tip: Use "Enhancer Ready" for auto-improved prompts

3

Get Your Result

Generation takes 15-45 seconds. Don't like it? Tweak the prompt and iterate. Each version builds on the last.

Premium 2K model • 4K upscale available
